Work from a window seat over Hanoi's train street, right inside Notion.

At the top of the page is a live pixel-art café window: a train rolls past every few minutes, a cat naps on the counter, the light follows the real time in Hanoi. Press play for music, switch between 5 stations, turn the rain on when you need to focus.

**What's inside**

- ☕ A live café window with music: Acoustic, Deep Focus, Sax Lounge, Lo-fi and Study Beats
- 🚃 Hanoi's train street, or 8 more places: Da Lat, Saigon, Hue, Da Nang, Nha Trang and the Mekong
- 🌧 Rain toggle with rain-on-the-awning sounds
- 🌤 Today: your top 3 and small tasks for breaks
- ⏳ Today's phins: plan 15, 25 or 45-minute sittings, one task each
- 🌙 End of day: three short questions to close the day
- 📅 This week: one goal per day

**How it works**

1. Get it for free and open the Notion link
2. Click "Duplicate" in the top right
3. Press ▶ on the café window and start working

Want the full café? Hit "Open the café ↗" for the phin timer with a to-do slip, a keepsake shelf the cat fills after every phin, a diary wall with a question of the day, and a table to work alongside friends.

**FAQ**

- *Do I need an account for the music?* No. It plays right inside Notion.
- *Can I pick another city?* Yes. Add `?place=dalat` (or another place) to the window link. The list is in the template.
- *Does it work on mobile?* Yes, in the Notion app. The station buttons hide on narrow screens, the play and next buttons stay.
- *I duplicated the old version. Do I need to redo it?* No, the window updates by itself. Duplicate again only if you want the new sections.
- *Who made the art and music?* Designed by Bin Nguyen, a product designer in Vietnam. The pixel art and music are made with AI tools.
