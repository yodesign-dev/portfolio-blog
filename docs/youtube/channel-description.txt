Pull up a stool. You've found a little pixel café in Vietnam.

Lofi Pixel Cafe is lofi music for studying, working and slowing down, played from a window seat somewhere in Vietnam. A train squeezes down Hanoi's train street. Rain taps the awning in Da Lat. Morning light spills over the rice fields of the Mekong. Inside, a phin coffee drips, someone types at the counter, and the café cat sleeps through all of it.

Every video is a long, calm session: 1–2 hours of lofi, jazzhop and acoustic beats, gentle from the first track to the last. Put it on, start one task, and let the café keep you company.
